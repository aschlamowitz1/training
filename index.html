01
Habit beats motivation. Motivation is weather. Habit is climate. You said it — you're a creature of habit, and that wins more often than not.
02
A meal is not a binge. Eating a planned, intentional meal — even a big one — is not the same thing as losing control. Don't conflate them. Don't punish them the same way.
03
Structure protects against overcorrection. Don't restrict the day after to "make up." Don't add cardio out of guilt. Stick to the plan. The plan is the plan because it works.
04
The data is one input, not a verdict. A 38% recovery is information. A 240.3 reading is information. Neither is a judgment about you. Trends over time, not snapshots.
05
No caffeine after 11 AM. Permanent rule. Data-validated multiple times. Late caffeine costs you HRV, sleep depth, and the next day's recovery. Not negotiable.
06

07
